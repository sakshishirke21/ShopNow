Billing & charges

  • You're only charged once your order is confirmed at checkout.
  • A payment receipt is emailed to you after every successful order.
  • You can view a printable invoice for any order from Your Orders.
  • If a payment fails but money was deducted, it's usually auto-refunded within 5-7 business days.
Having trouble with a charge or refund? Contact our support team and include your order number.
