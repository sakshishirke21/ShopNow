Digital services & device support

Help using the ShopNow website and app on your devices.

🌐

Browser issues

Try clearing your browser cache or switching browsers if pages aren't loading correctly.

📶

Slow loading

Check your internet connection - product images and pages need a stable connection to load.

🔑

Sign-in trouble

Make sure you're using the email you registered with. If you're still stuck, contact support.

🛒

Cart or checkout errors

Refresh the page and try again. If an item won't add to cart, it may be out of stock.

🔔

Notifications

Order updates are sent to the email on your account. Check your spam folder if you don't see them.

🖥️

Supported devices

ShopNow works on modern desktop and mobile browsers - no separate app install required.

Still need help? Contact our support team.
